# Drifting Guests

Paint invisible currents. Jellyfish drift through the water you set in motion.

## Interaction
Drag to paint a current. Tap for bioluminescence. Currents remain until you calm them.

Model: Flow-field painting and buoyancy. Scene-specific input, persistent state and simulation are in assets/art/interactions.js. Rendering and interpolation are shared; there is no generic touch-repulsion interaction.
