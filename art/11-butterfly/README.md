# A Place to Land

Offer a perch. A butterfly flutters toward it, then slowly opens its wings.

## Interaction
Tap to grow a perch. Hold still to let the butterfly land. Drag to lead it elsewhere.

Model: Flight, following and perching. Scene-specific input, persistent state and simulation are in assets/art/interactions.js. Rendering and interpolation are shared; there is no generic touch-repulsion interaction.
