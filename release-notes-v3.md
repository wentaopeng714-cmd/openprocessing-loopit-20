Twenty scene-specific interaction models replace generic touch-repulsion. Feed fish; sculpt mountains; plant and water flowers; draw aurora ribbons; pour tea; pet a cat; paint currents; weave threads; rake sand; attract a butterfly; wipe fog; scratch records; shake leaves; reveal lunar phases; slice fruit; fold paper wings; create gravity wells; pull a lamp cord; steer boats; sculpt expressions.

The entire current interface is English. Artwork pages have no source-code links and no universal scatter, regroup or color-change controls. Current standalone packages contain only the new studies and their required source and attribution files.
