Author: Vamoss; inspiration Aida Inma

Original notices remain in the code. Source file URLs and SHA256 hashes are in source.json.
Adaptation: Tap a tile; drag a chain; rotate every tile in a wave; shuffle or change ink.
Retained visual algorithm: Hexagonal arc masks with interpolated sixty-degree tile rotations
