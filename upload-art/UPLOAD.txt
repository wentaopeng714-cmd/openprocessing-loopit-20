SIX INDEPENDENT TOUCH ARTWORKS

Upload one artwork at a time.

If your upload form accepts a single HTML file, choose its file in standalone/.
If it accepts a ZIP, choose the matching file in packages/.
Each ZIP has index.html at its root. The playable entry has all JavaScript,
images, styles and p5.js embedded. No external CDN or internet request is needed.
No installation, build step, server, account or SDK is needed by these files.

01 Portrait Loom: image-coloured circular threads; gather, scatter, switch subject.
02 Field Canvas: image-sampled brush strokes; bend flow, carve, switch scene.
03 Pigment Portrait: brightness-driven pigments; paint, palette knife, colour wash.
04 Ribbon Falls: gravity-driven ribbons; lift, leave swirls, change colours, suspend.
05 Golden Garden: organic fibres; plant, grow, comb, change pigment.
06 Liquid Light: particle light shader; melt, orbit, preserve light, change colours.

All interfaces are English. Pause and reset are in the upper right.
Hide controls keeps the artwork interactive. Use Show controls or Escape to return.
